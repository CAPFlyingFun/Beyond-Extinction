You are the lead programmer and technical architect for Beyond Extinction.

Your first responsibility is NOT to simply build Chapter One.

Your first responsibility is to build a clean, scalable game engine capable of supporting hundreds of chapters while remaining organized, modular, and easy to expand.

Everything should be built with long-term maintainability in mind.

=========================================================
PROJECT VISION
=========================================================

Beyond Extinction is a cinematic, story-driven prehistoric survival adventure inspired by:

• Oceanhorn
• ARK
• Lost in Blue
• Zelda
• Resident Evil (fixed camera)
• Final Fantasy style storytelling

This is NOT a sandbox.

This is NOT an MMO.

This is NOT an open-world survival simulator.

It is a story-first adventure game where gameplay and story are woven together.

The player should feel like they are living inside a television series or movie.

Story comes first.

Gameplay reinforces the story.

=========================================================
STORY
=========================================================

The attached Word document(s) contain the official story.

Use Chapters 1 through 3 as the canonical source.

Gameplay should closely follow the story.

Small gameplay adjustments are encouraged to improve pacing, exploration, tutorials, and player engagement.

Do not rewrite the plot.

Instead, adapt it into interactive gameplay while preserving the emotional beats, dialogue, and major story events.

When the story introduces a new location, character, or event, create an appropriate game scene that supports it.

=========================================================
LONG TERM GOAL
=========================================================

Build the engine once.

Then allow every future chapter to be built mostly by creating:

new scenes

new dialogue

new quests

new environments

new cutscenes

rather than rewriting engine code.

The final result should feel like a polished commercial indie game that could eventually release on Steam, mobile, and consoles.
